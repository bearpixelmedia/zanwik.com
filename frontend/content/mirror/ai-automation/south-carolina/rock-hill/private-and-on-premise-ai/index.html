Private AI in York County

For firms whose client work can't leave the office.

Rock Hill's professional economy runs on confidentiality: client files, contract terms, personnel records, and work product firms are obliged to protect. Plenty of owners have looked at cloud AI, read their client agreements, and closed the tab.

Private AI removes the tradeoff. The models run on a machine in your building, documents stay on your network, and your team still gets drafting, answers, and automation. It's also the answer when a client's security questionnaire asks where their data goes.

That holds across Rock Hill: Old Town and downtown, the Winthrop area, the Galleria corridor, India Hook and Newport, and across York County in Fort Mill, Tega Cay, York, Clover, and Lake Wylie.

How private AI runs

Real AI help, inside your walls.

01

Runs in your building

The models live on hardware in your office or shop, sized to your workload. Nothing is sent out to be answered.
02

Built for strict rules

Client confidentiality, contract terms, and industry rules designed in from the start: permissions per person, and records of everything the system touches.
03

Your team still gets the help

Drafting, summaries, and answers from your Company Brain, which lives on the same machine: your policies and past work, searchable by your team and nobody else.
04

Approval steps and audit trails

Anything sensitive stops for a person, and every action is logged, so you can show a client or a regulator exactly what happened.
05

No cloud AI bill, no changed terms

No per-seat subscriptions creeping up, no vendor quietly changing what it does with your data. The machine is yours and the terms are physical.
06

We keep it running

Updates, tuning, and fixes are on us, on site or remotely, as part of Stay. A private system shouldn't need a private IT department.

AI and data rules in South Carolina.

We found no broad AI law or consumer-privacy law for South Carolina private businesses as of September 2026. Federal and industry rules still apply.

Keep sensitive customer data out of public AI tools unless your vendor contract covers it.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is on-premise AI as capable as the big cloud tools?

For the work most businesses actually do, drafting, summarizing, searching your records, answering from your documents, yes. For a few frontier tasks the cloud models are ahead, and we'll tell you plainly where that line falls for your work.

What hardware does it take?

Usually one dedicated machine, sized to how much you ask of it. We spec it in the Blueprint with real prices, and it lives on your network under your roof.

Do you work with businesses in Rock Hill and York County?

Yes. Zanwik is based in Clyde, North Carolina.

Our clients' agreements forbid uploading their files to cloud AI. Can we still use AI?

Yes. That's exactly the case private AI is built for. The files never leave your network, and we document that for your clients in writing.

Can you install it on site?

Yes. Installation, training, and the monthly check-ins happen at your place, anywhere in York County.

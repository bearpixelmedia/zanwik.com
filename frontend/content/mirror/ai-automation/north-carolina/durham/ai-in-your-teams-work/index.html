Rules to know

AI and data rules in North Carolina.

We found no broad AI law or consumer-privacy law for North Carolina private businesses as of September 2026. Federal and industry rules still apply.

Keep sensitive customer data out of public AI tools unless your vendor contract covers it.

This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice.

Free help in Durham County

Programs worth knowing.

NC Small Business & Technology Development Center (SBTDC)

Free, confidential advising for North Carolina small businesses: planning, capital, operations. Find them at sbtdc.org.

SCORE Durham

Free mentoring from folks who've run businesses, through SCORE's Durham chapter.

Greater Durham Chamber of Commerce

The city's business network: programs, advocacy, and the fastest way to meet other owners in Durham County.

Questions

Straight answers.

Which AI tools do you set up?

The ones that fit your stack and your rules. We're not a reseller for any of them; the Ops Spec names the tool, the cost, and why it won, in writing.

Is our company information safe in these tools?

Set up properly, yes: business-grade accounts with data protections, clear rules on what goes in, and private or on-premise options for the most sensitive work. The setup is the difference, and it's most of what we do here.

Do you work with businesses in Durham and Durham County?

Yes. We work with Durham businesses remotely, from first call to launch, and we're glad to come on site when a project calls for it. Zanwik is based in Western North Carolina.

Our team isn't technical. Will they actually use it?

That's why it lives inside the tools they already use and the training is on their real tasks. Adoption is part of the build, and Stay checks usage monthly, not once at launch.

How fast will we see a difference?

Drafting and lookup help usually shows up in the first week. The honest gains get measured in the first Stay report, in hours, against your own baseline.

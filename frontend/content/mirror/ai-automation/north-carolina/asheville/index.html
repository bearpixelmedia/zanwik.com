Buncombe County's business base

A visitor economy and a working city, side by side.

Buncombe County had 9,863 business locations in 2023, and 2,487 had 10 to 499 employees. Restaurants and food service led that band with 518 locations and 16,387 jobs across the county. Retail followed with 397 in the band and 19,291 jobs.

The working side is large too. The county counted 1,225 professional and technical firms, 964 construction firms and 328 manufacturers. Those manufacturers employed 13,301 people. Rules to know

AI and data rules in North Carolina.

As of September 2026, North Carolina has not passed a broad AI law or consumer-privacy law that applies to private businesses. Federal rules and your industry's rules still apply.

If you handle health, financial, or children's data, keep it out of public AI tools unless your vendor contract covers it. Private, on-site AI is an option when it can't leave the building.

This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice.

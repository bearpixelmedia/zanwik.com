Rules to know

AI and data rules in North Carolina.

As of September 2026, North Carolina has not passed a broad AI law or consumer-privacy law that applies to private businesses. Federal rules and your industry's rules still apply.

If you handle health, financial, or children's data, keep it out of public AI tools unless your vendor contract covers it. Private, on-site AI is an option when it can't leave the building.

This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do we have to replace our software first?

Rarely. Most tools Asheville firms run can be cleaned and connected where they sit. If one can't carry the load, you'll hear it before you spend.

What happens to our old data?

We clean it and move it, and keep the originals until you've checked the new copy. Nothing gets deleted on our word alone.

Do we need clean data before AI?

Yes, and that's most of the work. Give an agent three conflicting records and it guesses. Give it one clean record and a Company Brain built on it, and it answers.

How long does a foundation project take?

Usually two to six weeks, depending on the number of systems and the state of the records. The Ops Spec puts the timeline in writing first.

Do you work with Asheville and Buncombe County businesses?

Yes. We work with Asheville businesses remotely, from first call to launch, and we're glad to come on site when a project calls for it. Zanwik is based in Western North Carolina.

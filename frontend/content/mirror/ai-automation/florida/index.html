What we build here

The slow spots are the same in every Florida business.

Whatever you run, the drag looks alike: follow-up that slips, quotes that take days, and numbers that live in three places. Here's what we build to fix it. A person approves anything that matters.

01

The diagnostic and the Ops Spec

We map how your business actually runs, find the work that's costing you the most, and put a dollar figure on it. The Zanwik Ops Spec is the written plan that comes out of it: what to build, in what order, and what it should earn you, for a fixed fee starting at $1,500. It's yours to keep whether we build it or someone else does.
02

Systems and data foundation

Clean data and connected systems: one CRM instead of three, records that match across your tools, and integrations between the systems you already pay for. You can't put AI on top of a mess, so we fix the foundation first when it needs fixing.
03

AI inside your team's work

AI working inside the tools your people already use: drafting, summarizing, answering questions from your own company knowledge, and pulling reports, so your team spends its hours on judgment calls instead of busywork.
04

Agents that handle whole tasks

Agents that do whole jobs, not just answer questions: answering calls and inquiries, intake and booking, follow-up that doesn't slip, quotes and proposals drafted in hours, documents processed, invoices chased, scheduling handled, meeting notes turned into tasks, and reporting pulled together. A person approves the calls that matter.
05

Custom software and integrations

Portals, dashboards, configurators, and the connections between the systems you already pay for, built when the fix needs something that doesn't exist yet. Recent work includes a live product configurator for a custom builder, launched with nine models and real-time pricing.
06

Private and on-premise AI

AI that runs on hardware you own, inside your building, for work that's too sensitive for public AI tools. Your data never leaves your walls. Built for firms with strict privacy or compliance rules.
07

AI spend and token governance

A clear view of what your business spends on AI tools and tokens, where it's wasted, and limits that keep it in check. Model-agnostic, so you can switch AI models later without a rebuild.
08

Stay: we run what we built

We stay on to keep your systems running and improving, check the work, and report what it earned you in dollars. We own the architecture. You own the tools. If you ever stop, your tools and accounts stay with you.

Not limited to this list. Every engagement starts by assessing what your business actually needs, and we build whatever it requires.

Rules to know

AI and data rules in Florida.

Florida Digital Bill of Rights (in effect July 1, 2024): a privacy law that applies to very large companies (over $1 billion in revenue plus other conditions), so it generally doesn't apply to businesses our size.

A proposed Florida "AI Bill of Rights" (SB 482) passed the Senate in 2026 but not the House, so it is not law.

This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice.

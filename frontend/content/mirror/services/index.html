How does Zanwik's process work?

Three steps. Start: the free assessment (a few questions, score emailed) points to what's costing you the most, and the Ops Spec maps it. Build: we automate that work with AI agents and connected systems. Stay: a monthly retainer keeps it running, fixed, and under your control.

How long does implementation take?

Depends on scope. The Ops Spec, a fixed-fee starter build, can ship in weeks. Larger builds, like full CRM integration or multi-agent systems, are scoped and priced phase by phase, so you know the timeline before committing.

What if we already pay for a CRM or other software?

We connect what you already have (CRM, accounting, booking, job data) into one source of truth rather than making you rip and replace. Cutting Tool Sprawl is also its own service if you're paying for tools that overlap.

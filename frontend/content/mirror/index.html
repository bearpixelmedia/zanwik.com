In plain English

What Zanwik does, in one minute.

Every business has jobs that eat hours and slip through the cracks. We find them, fix them, and keep them fixed.

Step 1

Map the work that already costs money.

Quotes that go out late. Leads nobody follows up. Invoices that wait. Reports built by hand. We find the ones costing you the most and put a dollar figure on each.

Step 2

Wire the fix into tools you already pay for.

We build it inside the software you already use: your CRM, your phones, your inbox, your billing. No new platform to learn, and no rip and replace.

Step 3

Keep a Run Desk on it.

We monitor it, fix it when it breaks, and improve it every quarter. A person approves anything that matters, and you get a monthly report in dollars.

If you run the business

You get a plain-English plan, a fixed quote, and a monthly report in dollars. You never have to learn the technology to know it's working.

If you run the technology

Model-agnostic builds on your own accounts, so you can swap Claude, GPT, Gemini, or an open-weight model without a rebuild. Human approval on the calls that matter, with monitoring and documentation on every build. We own the architecture. You own the tools, the data, and the accounts.

Sound familiar?

Four ways a business leaks money without anyone noticing.

Leads cool off while inboxes sit.

A call rolls to voicemail. A quote request sits for two days. The customer calls someone else.

People retype what systems should pass.

Retyping the same information between systems. Chasing paperwork. Building the same report by hand every week.

Cost hides across tools and spreadsheets.

Software and AI subscriptions pile up, and nobody can say which ones earn their keep.

Know-how walks out with the next hire.

When one person is out or leaves, the answers go with them.

Stay

Most AI firms build it and leave. We keep a Run Desk on it.

The build is not the finish line. It's the handoff most firms walk away from. We don't.

When you stay with us, we run what we built: monitoring it, fixing it when it breaks, and improving it every quarter. You get a monthly proof report in dollars: what ran, what it cost, and what it earned. And you get a person accountable for all of it, not a ticket queue.

Add a fractional AI officer seat and you also get an owner for the roadmap: biweekly reviews, quarterly architecture checks, and someone whose job is deciding what gets built next, and what isn't worth building.

Every software subscription goes on your card, not ours. We don't resell tools or mark them up. Your data and your accounts stay yours, and the documentation ships with every build.

Straight answers.

What do you actually do?

We build AI into the whole operation. Agents that take whole jobs off your team's plate, your company's knowledge pulled into one layer every system can draw on, and custom software where the off-the-shelf tools run out. The everyday work is part of it (follow-up, quotes, scheduling, reporting), but it's the floor, not the pitch. Then the Run Desk keeps it earning, checked by a person, so nothing runs unsupervised.

What kinds of things can you build?

Almost anything that happens inside your operation: follow-up and quoting, intake, invoicing, reporting, custom software and customer portals, AI agents, one screen that shows the whole business, and more. The full list is above, and every item links to a page that explains it. If you're not sure which one you need, the Ops Check or a call will tell you. If something isn't worth building, we'll say so.

I'm not technical. Will I understand what I'm buying?

Yes. Everything we hand you is written in plain English: a written assessment after the call, an Ops Spec that shows what we'd build and what it's worth in dollars, and a monthly report in dollars once it's running. If you have technical people, they get the architecture and the documentation too.

Where should I start: the Ops Check, a call, or the Ops Spec?

Start with the Ops Check if you want a quick read on where you stand: a few questions, and your score is emailed to you. Book a call if you'd rather talk it through: 30 minutes, and you get a written assessment either way. Choose the Ops Spec when you already know you want a plan: a fixed-fee map of what to build, what it's worth, and what it costs.

What happens after the build is live?

We keep a Run Desk on it. Stay is our operating partner retainer: we run the systems we built, monitor them, fix them when they break, and improve them every quarter. You get a monthly proof report in dollars: what ran, what it cost, what it earned. It's the part of the engagement most firms skip, and it's the part we built this firm around.

How much does this cost?

Start is free: the Ops Check, and a 30-minute diagnostic call with a written assessment you keep either way. The Ops Spec is a fixed fee from $1,500, scoped to your operation and set on the free call before you commit to anything. The Build is a fixed quote from the Ops Spec, in writing, before anything starts. Stay is priced from the Ops Spec, once we know what we're running for you.

Which industries do you work in?

Manufacturing, construction, hospitality, real estate, and more. We start from the problem, not the industry: if work in your business moves through email, phones, spreadsheets, and people, we can usually help. The industries page has the detail.

Who owns the systems?

We own the architecture. You own the tools. Your data and your accounts stay yours, and your software subscriptions run on your card, in your name. Every build ships with documentation. The full terms sit in the agreement, and we go through them with you before anything is signed.

Do we have to switch software?

No. We build into the tools you already run: your CRM, your phones, your inbox, your billing. Switching software is usually the most expensive way to fix a work problem.

What if it's not a fit?

Then the diagnostic tells you so, and it costs you nothing: it's free, and the written assessment is yours either way. If we're not the right firm for the work, the Ops Check still shows you what to do next.

Do we need to already be using AI?

No. Most of our clients have tried AI tools and gotten little from them. We start with a diagnostic that finds the work actually costing you money, then build from there.

Where do you work?

Across the South. We work with Southern businesses remotely, from first call to launch, and we're glad to come on site when a project calls for it. Zanwik is based in Western North Carolina. Every state in the South is a focus market for us.

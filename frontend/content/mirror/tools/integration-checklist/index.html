Questions we hear

Checklist questions, answered

How long does the checklist take?

About 15 minutes if you know your systems, longer if you have to hunt down logins. That is useful information too: if you cannot answer a question, that box is doing its job.

Do I need every box checked before using AI?

No. But the unchecked boxes are your risk list. Most businesses start building with around 20 checked and a plan for the rest. The grade at the bottom tells you where you stand.

Who should fill this out?

The person who would get the call if something broke. Usually the owner or the office manager, not the vendor selling you the system.

Is my progress saved anywhere?

Only in your own browser. Your answers never leave your computer and we never see them.

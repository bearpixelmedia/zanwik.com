How the math works

  • Yearly cost = hours per week x 52 weeks x your hourly rate.
  • Fit score = volume score x repetitiveness score. Volume comes from your hours (6+ is high, 3+ is medium). Repetitiveness is our rating of how pattern-driven the task is. 7+ is High fit, 4+ is Medium.
  • Fix-first order = yearly cost x fit weight (High 1.5, Medium 1.0, Low 0.6). Biggest expensive-and-automatable wins rise to the top.

From you. We multiply the hours you enter by 52 weeks and your hourly rate. The full formula is shown under your results. No hidden math.

What an employee truly costs per hour: wages plus taxes, benefits, and overhead. Most small businesses land between $30 and $60. When in doubt, use $40.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your numbers never leave your computer and we never see them.

Why it exists

The bridge between “we should do AI” and a paid build — without guessing.

Most businesses know AI could help and have no idea where to start, what it would cost, or whether it would pay. So they stall, or they buy a tool that sits unused.

The Ops Spec removes the guesswork. For a fixed, scoped fee, you get a clear-eyed read on your whole operation and a concrete plan with real numbers attached. It is the cheapest, fastest way to find out exactly what a build would involve, before you commit to one.

It is a finished deliverable, not a deposit on a bigger sale. You pay a fair fixed price, you get a real plan, and it is yours to act on however you choose.

Questions

Straight answers.

How much does the Ops Spec cost?

From $1,500, scoped to the complexity of your operation. A small, owner-operated business sits at the entry point. A larger business with more systems and locations scopes higher. We set the exact number in writing before you commit to anything, so there are no surprises.

What's the difference between this and the free assessment?

The free assessment is a few questions, and your score is emailed to you. It shows whether there's a problem worth solving and roughly where it is. The Ops Spec is the deep, paid engagement that maps your whole operation, names what your biggest gap costs in dollars, and hands you a fixed scope and quote for the fix. The assessment points. The Ops Spec is the plan.

Why is it paid, not free?

Because it is real work with a real deliverable, not a sales pitch. The free assessment shows whether an Ops Spec is even worth commissioning. The Ops Spec itself is days of deep audit and architecture work that ends in a document you own. Charging a fair fixed fee means the people who commission one are serious, and that our work is respected.

Do I have to build with Zanwik after?

No. The Ops Spec is yours. The architecture map, the roadmap, and the build scope are documents you own and can hand to your own team or any builder. There is no lock-in. Most people build with us because the plan and the person who wrote it are the same, but you are never obligated.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks from kickoff, depending on the complexity we scope before kickoff. You give us access and an intake conversation, we do the deep work, and you get the document.

What you get

Four checkpoints. Every Ops Check.

Not a summary of what we discussed. A written read on your business that you can act on, whether or not you hire us to act on it.

01

Written stack assessment

Operations, pipeline, technology, and AI coverage: mapped in writing and delivered after the call. Not a summary of what you told us. A structured read of how your business actually runs, against what it could run like.

02

A named cost-of-inaction figure

We put a dollar figure on what staying still costs. Not a range, not a vague directional. A specific, defensible number built from your revenue systems, so the decision to act or not is made with full visibility into the trade-off.

03

The highest-ROI place to start

Not a list of everything you could do. One prioritized recommendation: the highest-leverage intervention in your specific operation, sequenced before anything else, with the reasoning made explicit.

04

A phased roadmap

A build plan where every phase is independently committable. Phase 1 is scoped and priced. Later phases are directional. You can stop after Phase 1 with no lock-in, no obligation, no pressure. The roadmap is a menu.

Questions

Straight answers.

Common ones, answered directly. If yours isn't here, email it.

Is the diagnostic really free?

Yes. The assessment is the door: it's how Zanwik gets to know your business and how you get to know how we think. If the diagnostic doesn't surface a problem worth solving, there's no engagement to have. We'd rather tell you that in thirty minutes than string it out.

Will you try to sell me on the call?

No pitch deck. No sales call. The call is structured around mapping your operation: we ask, you talk, we build the picture. The written assessment lands after the call, and you decide what to do with it. There's no high-pressure close built into this process.

Who is this for?

Any business that needs transformation and doesn't know where to start, most of all small and growing businesses. Nobody is too small: a $500K owner-operator with the right problem is a better fit than a $10M business without one. Nobody is too large. The diagnostic scales. If you run a business and feel operational drag, you're the right fit.

What do you need from me before the call?

Your website and one sentence on what the business does. That's it. We do the pre-call research: your digital footprint, competitive landscape, visible operational signals. You don't have to prepare a deck or come with a specific agenda. Just show up.

What happens if I want to move forward after the call?

The diagnostic itself is free, thirty minutes, no obligation. If the Ops Check reveals a problem worth solving, we scope the next step together, whether that is a fixed-fee Ops Spec or a Phase 1 build, and it's yours to commit to or not. Nothing in a Zanwik SOW locks you past the phase you've signed.

What comes after the call

If the problem is bigger than thirty minutes, the next step is the Ops Spec.

A fixed-fee deep audit of your operation: a written architecture map, a prioritized roadmap, the dollar figure on your biggest gap, and a fixed scope and quote for the build. From $1,500, scoped to your business. A finished plan you own, with us or without us.

Questions

Straight answers.

Common ones, answered directly. If yours isn't here, email it.

Why are the case studies anonymized?

Client confidentiality. Every number on this page is real, pulled from a live engagement, and labeled measured or modeled. The company names are withheld, not the results.

What's the difference between "measured" and "modeled" results?

Measured means we have direct data: logs, dashboards, before-and-after numbers. Modeled means the number is a reasonable estimate based on the scope of what was built, clearly labeled as such, and never presented as a measured result.

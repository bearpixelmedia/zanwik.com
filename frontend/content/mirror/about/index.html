About

Operators first. Architecture second. Slide decks never.

Zanwik is the embedded operating partner for businesses the consulting industry priced out and the agency world underbuilt. This is who we are, how we work, and why we exist.

The origin

Own the workflow layer — the piece between your tools and your team.

Byron. President. 23. Western North Carolina.

I graduated from the University of Mississippi in May 2025 with a degree in Management Information Systems. First move: Apex Systems, a consulting and staffing firm. I sat inside the enterprise CRM world: Dynamics 365, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho. I learned big business from the inside, learned how the machine runs, learned what transformation means when it is being sold to a Fortune 500 procurement committee instead of delivered to an operator who needs it done.

I was miserable.

“Every single day I sat in that chair, I felt it. This isn't mine. I'm building someone else's thing on someone else's timeline for someone else's vision.”

So I left and built Zanwik. Sole proprietor, operating out of Western North Carolina. No clients, no team, no playbook. Just the conviction that there was a massive gap between what small and growing businesses needed and what existed to serve them. Closing that gap was worth betting on.

What followed was twelve months of figuring it out: building on live client engagements, developing the methodology, recruiting the team, refining the thesis. Not theoretical. Not academic. Done in the field, on real businesses, with real stakes.

“I learned more in twelve months than I did in four years of school. I'm figuring it out on my terms, and that makes all the difference.”

The thesis

Own the middle layer.

Not the model. The architecture between the model and how the business uses it.

The thesis crystallized in April 2026, when a mentor sent four articles with one directive: The time is now.

The framing that stuck was simple: enterprise has dedicated AI platforms, a company built for Fortune 500 budgets. Mid-market operators need the same class of wiring without the enterprise price — thathis work at Fortune 500 scale. The small and growing business has nobody. No embedded architect. No one owning the intelligence layer. No one building the system that connects the AI to how the business actually runs.

“Own the workflow layer — between your tools and your team.”

The gap is structural: enterprise has capital, procurement cycles, and vendor relationships that support dedicated AI infrastructure teams. The small and growing business has momentum, owner-operator urgency, and a total absence of anyone building their middle layer. That market is not small. It is the largest segment of the US economy, and every well-funded firm in this space ignores it. Zanwik walks straight in.

What we believe

Five operating values. No filler.

Not wall-poster words. The standards every engagement is measured against, including by us.

01

Precision

Every engagement starts with a specific, quantified read of what's broken, modeled in dollars and not hunches. Vague discomfort is not a problem statement. A named cost-of-inaction figure is.
02

Structure

Systems, not heroics. Every problem is diagnosable and designable. If we can't draw the architecture, we don't understand it well enough to build it.
03

Execution

We ship. Deliverables go live before contracts are fully countered. The test isn't 'did we advise well.' It's 'what is live and running in your business right now.'
04

Ownership

We own the architecture. You own the tools. Tool subscriptions go on your card. When an engagement ends, you walk away with the tools, the accounts, and full documentation to operate what was built. The retainer is the ongoing license to keep the architectural IP maintained and evolving inside your operation.
05

Impact

The measure isn't hours logged. It's whether the business runs differently afterward. A Zanwik engagement should be visible in the P&L within 90 days of close.

How we make money

We sell the architecture. You buy the tools.

Every engagement is custom. The relationship is maintenance and evolution.

Zanwik's invoice covers the IP: the architecture, the workflow design, the custom builds, the AI agents, and the ongoing maintenance and evolution that keeps the systems compounding. That is what we charge for.

Everything else, including the CRM subscription, the automation platform, the AI model access, and the hosting, goes on your card, paid directly to the vendor. You own those relationships. When we exit, you own your stack. There is no vendor dependency on Zanwik that makes it harder to leave.

Questions

Straight answers.

Common ones, answered directly. If yours isn't here, email it.

Who is Zanwik built for?

Businesses with 11 to 500 employees: too big for a freelancer's Trello board, too small for a firm like McKinsey or Bain to take the work.

Is Zanwik a consulting firm or an agency?

Neither. Consultants advise and leave. Agencies execute without staying to run it. We design, build, and run the systems, and we stay embedded as long as the engagement makes sense.

Who's actually doing the work?

Byron architects every engagement directly, and Carson Turner owns every technical build. You're not handed off to a junior account team.

Where do you work?

Across the South. We work with Southern businesses remotely, from first call to launch, and we're glad to come on site when a project calls for it. Zanwik is based in Western North Carolina. Every state in the South is a focus market for us.
